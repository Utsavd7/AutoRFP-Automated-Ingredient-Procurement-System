# Product

## Register

product

## Users

QuotePlate serves independent and small chain restaurants in India. The primary users are owners, managers, chefs, and purchasing staff who may be working from a phone, may not use procurement terminology, and need to buy ingredients quickly without losing control of supplier relationships.

## Product Purpose

QuotePlate turns menus and ingredient needs into one clear request, sends that request to the restaurant's chosen suppliers, compares the complete returned prices, and keeps the restaurant's final decision and buying history in one place. Success means a first time visitor can understand the flow in seconds and a restaurant team can complete a real buying cycle without training.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, and accountable. QuotePlate should feel like a dependable restaurant operations tool: direct enough for a busy kitchen manager, credible enough for an owner, and calm enough for repeated daily work.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI SaaS landing page, an enterprise procurement suite filled with jargon, a supplier marketplace, or a decorative restaurant website. Avoid invented customer numbers, guaranteed savings, abstract AI imagery, excessive cards, gratuitous motion, and technical security language.

## Design Principles

1. Show the buying journey instead of describing it in long paragraphs.
2. Use familiar restaurant language before procurement terminology.
3. Keep the restaurant in control of suppliers and every final decision.
4. Make privacy boundaries visible, especially for menus, recipes, prices, and supplier access.
5. Use realistic Indian restaurant examples and label every illustrative record honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use readable English, strong contrast, large touch targets, semantic structure, visible focus states, and a complete reduced motion experience. The landing page must remain understandable on small phones, tablets, laptops, slow connections, and when animation is unavailable.
